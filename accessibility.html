Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Our Commitment

Just For Phishing should be usable by everyone who needs the training, including people who navigate by keyboard, use a screen reader, or need reduced motion. This page describes what the site does today and what it does not yet do — it is a status report, not a marketing claim.

What works today

⌨️ Keyboard Navigation

Every page starts with a skip link. Quiz answers are real buttons you can reach with Tab and choose with Enter or Space.

🎨 Color Contrast

Body and label text meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) against the page background. Verified by measurement, not by eye.

🎬 Reduced Motion

With prefers-reduced-motion set, scroll animations and the quiz's decorative emoji are switched off and all content is shown immediately.

📱 Responsive Design

Every module works at phone width. No page scrolls sideways.

Details

  • Landmarks: every page has a <main> region and a skip link as its first tabbable element.
  • Focus indicators: a visible focus ring on every focusable element, shown for keyboard users and suppressed for mouse users via :focus-visible.
  • Menu state: the mobile menu reports aria-expanded, closes on Escape, and returns focus to the button that opened it.
  • Target size: standalone links and buttons are at least 24×24 CSS pixels.
  • Alt text: images carry descriptive alternatives; decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Color independence: correct and incorrect answers are labelled in text, not signalled by color alone.

Known Limitations

These are real gaps we have not closed yet. If one of them blocks you, please tell us — it moves up the list.

  • The site has not been tested end to end with NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver. Announcements are verified programmatically, which is not the same as hearing them.
  • Some training content — email headers, raw sender addresses — is dense by design, and we have not yet checked how it reads aloud line by line.
  • The site has not been audited by an independent accessibility specialist.

Recently fixed

Listed here because this page previously described these as limitations:

  • Every module is now keyboard-operable. Answers are real buttons, reachable with Tab and activated with Enter or Space.
  • Results announce themselves. Each module speaks its verdict through a live region, and focus moves to the result rather than being dropped back to the top of the page.
  • Phish or Treat's timer can be extended or switched off. There is a "Practise without the timer" option and an "Add 2 minutes" button.
  • Text contrast meets AA. The muted text colour used for labels and captions was previously well below the threshold.

Conformance Status

Partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. "Partially conformant" means we believe the site meets the standard but have not had that confirmed by testing with real assistive technology or by an independent audit — see Known Limitations. Every page and every training module has been checked for keyboard operation, focus handling, contrast and motion preferences.

Feedback & Assistance

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of Just For Phishing. If you encounter accessibility barriers:

  • Visit our Contact page to report issues
  • Open an issue on GitHub with the "accessibility" label
  • Describe the specific issue and the steps to reproduce it
  • Include information about your assistive technology (if applicable)

Compatibility

Built and tested against current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, with keyboard-only navigation and browser zoom to 200%. Screen reader support is a goal rather than a tested claim — see Known Limitations.

Third-Party Content

Some pages may link to external resources. We are not responsible for the accessibility of third-party websites.

Continuous Improvement

This is an open-source project, and we encourage contributions that improve accessibility. If you're a developer interested in helping make Just For Phishing more accessible, please visit our GitHub repository.

Contact Us

For accessibility questions or to report issues, please contact us. We typically respond within 3-5 business days.
